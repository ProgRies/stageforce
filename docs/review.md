# Prüfen

**Prüfen** collects organisations that need a follow-up email after a call. Tapping **E-Mail nötig** on a call adds the point "E-Mail senden"; the sidebar shows how many organisations are open.

Each row shows the organisation, what is open, and the latest timeline entry with its date and author, so the list alone usually says what to do. Selecting a row opens the organisation as usual.

A point disappears on its own once an outgoing email to that organisation is saved in the CRM, from the browser extension or the .eml import. Other open points stay. **Erledigt** in the organisation view closes every open point by hand and records that in the change log.

There are no automatic field checks any more: missing FastBill links, phone numbers or contradictory statuses no longer put an organisation on this list.
